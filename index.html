04 / Method

How I work

01

AI-assisted, not AI-authored

Models draft my boilerplate, editor scaffolding and test content, so I spend the day on the parts that need judgement. Every generated line gets read, reduced and owned before it ships.

02

Touch a value twice, build a tool

Design work slows down when tuning is expensive. I’d rather lose a morning to an editor window than lose a month to reopening the same script for one number.

03

Systems get tuned in play

I write the model on paper, then find the real numbers with hands on the controller — sliders live, one variable at a time, notes on what the change did to the fifteenth minute.

05 / About

About

I’m a Game Design student at HTW Berlin who ended up on the technical side because that’s where the interesting knobs are. I care most about systems: how a rule set produces surprise on the fifteenth minute, not the first.

Day to day I program, make technical art when a scene needs it, and build the editor tooling my teams tune with. I work with AI in the pipeline the way I work with any tool — hard, sceptically, and with my name on the result.
